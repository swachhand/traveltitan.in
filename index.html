Hi, I'm Altitude Ecstasy

An adventurer of the rugged Himalayan landscape

With more than 15 years of experience, I am a passionate hiker who has explored the tranquil valleys of the Indian Himalayas. I started my journey as a traveller in Himachal Pradesh, but over time, my curiosity to explore the unexplored has driven multiple journeys into the heart of the mountains. In the last five years, my focus has been on better understanding the landscape and culture of Uttarakhand.

Over the years, I have learnt to perform treks independently, finding my way across the isolated areas and dwellings of the Indian Himalayas. This was only possible due to extensive knowledge of topographic maps, navigational skills, mapping tools, ultra-light gear, and meteorological awareness refined over years of hiking.

Beyond the excitement of adventure, the mountains, meadows, rivers, and people hold a revered place in my heart. Through meaningful interactions and cultural exchanges, I have developed a profound regard and admiration for the Uttarakhand region's rich tapestry of human legacy, which includes distinctive traditions, temples, food, and way of life.

Why trek and hike?

In the quiet rhythm of the mountains, walking becomes more than physical movement—it becomes a journey inward. Surrounded by immense peaks and ancient trails, trekking naturally strips away our everyday distractions, grounding us in deep humility and patience. Each steady ascent invites us to pause, listen to our breath, and genuinely introspect. Far from the relentless rush of daily chores, the trail offers a rare sanctuary to reflect on who we are and find quiet clarity in sync with the natural world.

Undoubtedly, trekking is a holistic therapeutic. Unleash your adventurous spirit and explore new contours with me.
